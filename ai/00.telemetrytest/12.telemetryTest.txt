Step-by-Step Workflow
Step 1: Record a Walk in the App
1.
Open the app on your Android device.
2.
Tap Start Tracking and walk your route.
3.
Tap Stop Tracking. A Toast will confirm: Saved 85 telemetry events to: telemetry_20261011_153000.csv
Step 2: Transfer the CSV File to Your Development Computer
Connect your device via USB or WiFi ADB and run:
bash
adb pull /sdcard/Android/data/com.example.pdrandroutetest/files/telemetry/ .
Step 3: Add to Test Resources & Replay
Place the downloaded CSV into your project's test resources folder (app/src/test/resources/telemetry/):
kotlin
@Test
fun `replay recorded walk telemetry`() {
    val stream = javaClass.classLoader?.getResourceAsStream("telemetry/telemetry_20261011_153000.csv")
        ?: error("Telemetry file not found")
    val csvText = stream.bufferedReader().use { it.readText() }

    val events = TelemetryRecorder.fromCsvString(csvText)
    val history = replaySession(events)
    val finalState = history.last()

    assertTrue("EKF must be initialized", finalState.isInitializedWithGps)
    assertTrue("Position variance must stay bounded", finalState.position.variance < 50.0)
